Tools for your next step

Use these free tools right here. Nothing you type into them is sent to our servers. External sites open only when you choose an external link.

Privacy first: These tools work in your browser. Avoid entering Social Security numbers, criminal history, financial account numbers, or other sensitive details. Download or print your work before leaving. Resume text is not automatically saved.

Housing affordability calculator

Estimate how much rent you can manage after essential expenses. This is a planning estimate, not a landlord's eligibility decision.

Enter your monthly amounts to estimate a rent budget.

The estimate uses take-home pay, subtracts essentials, utilities, and your cushion, and compares the remainder with 30% of take-home pay. The 30% figure is a general budgeting reference, not a rule or guarantee.

Simple resume builder

Build a skills-focused resume. You can print or save it as a PDF using your browser. Nothing is uploaded to SuccessfulFelons.

Resume drafts save automatically on this device. Avoid sensitive personal information.

Tip: Use clear achievements and accurate experience. You do not need to include criminal-history information on a resume.

Application follow-up planner

Estimate follow-up dates and prepare a short message. Use My progress to record applications locally on this device.

Fill out the form to create a follow-up plan.

This is a suggested follow-up, not a guarantee of a response. Follow any timing instructions from the employer.

Independent income calculator

Estimate monthly profit from a service, freelance gig, or small business.

Enter your estimates to calculate revenue, expenses, and profit.

Profit here is before taxes, benefits, and personal expenses. Actual results can differ.

Application tracker

Keep a simple list of applications, next steps, and dates. Records stay in this browser only and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Do not include private case or identification numbers.

Housing assistance navigator

Find likely starting points based on your needs. This guide does not determine program eligibility or guarantee openings. You choose whether to open an official external resource.

Choose your needs to see a practical action plan.

Save and restore your progress

Your application tracker is saved automatically on this device. You can also download an offline backup and restore it later on another device. This is not online account syncing; secure accounts are still being built.

Backups may contain resume contact information, employer names, application dates and your notes. Store downloaded files somewhere private. Only import a backup you trust. Nothing is uploaded to our servers by these controls.
